What the values mean. Each point is the number of people counted in one interval (a few minutes), not a running total. Incoming counts people passing the line in the entering direction, outgoing in the leaving direction.

The counter reports a cumulative total once a minute. The values here are the difference between consecutive readings, summed per interval; if the counter drops (a reset), the new reading counts as the increase. The first reading in the database has nothing to compare with and counts as zero.

The tiles add up every interval in the selected range. Last reading is the time stamped by the counter, and received is when the server got it. Times are shown in your local time zone.
